Built from researchers for research labs

YouScope has driven automated microscopes for over a decade started in 2009, developed in the {{ site.affiliation.group }} group at {{ site.affiliation.dept }}, {{ site.affiliation.city }}. Learn more about the contributors.

Open and extendable

Free and open-source under a dual license Apache 2.0 or LGPL 2.1. Scriptable, plugin-based, and compatible with most present-day automated microscopes. View the code.

Reproducible by design

The seperation between hardware configuration and abstract imaging protocols allows for reproducibility by design, so an experiment can be re-run or shared exactly as it ran the first time even on different hardware.
